ABOUT / PARTNERSHIPS

About Echo Hollow

A place-based memory exchange connecting experiences through locations and time.

✦ PLACE FIRST · IDENTITY SECOND

Echo Hollow is not designed to connect people through profiles, but to connect their experiences through places and time.

Echo Hollow is a web-based platform supported by physical interactive installations. It is designed for people who are temporarily living in, visiting, or passing through a place — including tourists, new arrivals and people whose relationship with a city is still forming.

Users can leave memories, small discoveries, travel notes and personal thoughts, then encounter stories left by others who occupied the same place at another moment. Images remain place-focused: the system is designed to reject identifiable faces and keep attention on environments, objects and shared experiences.
PHASE 01

Public & social partners

Launch in high-traffic public locations through councils, universities, community groups, cultural organisations and other social partners.

PHASE 02

Local host network

Expand through compact Echo Hollow terminals hosted by cafés, bookstores and independent businesses through an installation and maintenance service model.

CONTENT

Memories + travel notes

Posts can be emotional reflections, local discoveries, short travel stories or messages left for someone in the future.

PRIVACY

No face-led sharing

Preset identities and place-focused image moderation reduce pressure to perform personal identity like conventional social media.

Bring Echo Hollow to your space.

We work with public organisations, community spaces and local businesses interested in hosting an Echo Hollow terminal or collaborating on a place-based installation.
